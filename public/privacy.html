Privacy policy

Vidura Life · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: Vidura Life asks for your name and email. Your email is used to confirm it's really yours. Everything else you do in the app, including your focus areas, meals, activity history, cycle details and lab-range settings, stays on your device and is never sent to us. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What we collect, and why

  • Your name and email address. Your name personalises the app. Your email is sent to Google Firebase Authentication, which emails you a one-tap link to confirm the address belongs to you and keeps a record that you signed in.
  • A contact record (name and email) may be saved in the Vidura Life contacts database so we can keep in touch about the app. We never sell or share it.

What stays on your device

Your focus areas (such as cholesterol, blood sugar, thyroid or fertility), food and kitchen preferences, age band, meals, completed activities, cycle details, voice and music settings and reminders are stored only on your phone or in your browser. We can't see them.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders in the iOS app, they are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server sends them and nothing about them leaves your device.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase Authentication, to confirm your email (see Google's privacy policy).
  • Google Firebase Hosting, which serves the website and its images and sounds. Like any web server, it briefly processes your IP address to deliver them.

Health information

Vidura Life gives general wellness information and educational reference ranges, not medical advice. You never enter lab results into the app, and any health interests you choose stay on your device.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Your data → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your email sign-in, your contact record and everything stored on the device. Clear my data removes only what's on the device.

Children

Vidura Life is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
